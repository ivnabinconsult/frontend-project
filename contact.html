Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping (2–3 days) is available at checkout for most items.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return window on all unused items in original packaging. Returns are free for defective or incorrect items.

Do you offer assembly services?

Yes, white-glove delivery and assembly is available for furniture items in select metro areas for an additional fee.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. Contact support immediately for assistance.

Do you ship internationally?

We currently ship to the US, Canada, UK, and EU. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout.

Are your products covered by warranty?

All electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Furniture is covered for structural defects for 2 years.
